Tester Guide

Thanks for helping test SoundPilot! This page has everything you need — about three minutes to read.

What you're testing

SoundPilot is an Android app that automatically switches your phone's sound settings — volume and Do Not Disturb — when you connect your Bluetooth headphones or speaker, and switches them back when you disconnect.

Example: you connect your earbuds → the phone gets louder and silences calls. You take them off → everything goes back to normal. By itself.

What you need

  • An Android phone — Android 8 or newer, so roughly any phone from 2018 onwards.
  • Any Bluetooth audio device: earbuds, headphones, a speaker, or your car.
  • Your own Google account on that phone — the one you use for Google Play.

How to join — one time, 5 minutes

  1. Sergey will send you a personal invitation link.
  2. Open the link in a browser on your phone and tap “Become a tester”. Make sure you're signed in with your usual Google account.
  3. On the same page, follow the link to Google Play and install SoundPilot — just like any normal app.

One important rule: the test runs for 14 days, and Google requires every tester to stay in for the whole time. Please don't leave the testing program or uninstall the app until Sergey says the test is finished — otherwise your spot is lost and can't be recovered.

First setup — 5 minutes

  1. Open the app and allow what it asks for (access to Do Not Disturb, Bluetooth). Without these permissions it simply can't do its job.
  2. Create two sound profiles — for example “Normal” (your everyday settings) and “Headphones” (louder, calls silenced).
  3. Link your headphones or speaker to a profile: pick the device in the app and choose which profile should turn on when it connects.

What to do during the 14 days

Just use it as part of your normal life — ideally a little every day:

  • Connect and disconnect your Bluetooth device the way you always do. Check that the profile switches on by itself and switches back when you disconnect.
  • Sometimes switch profiles manually from the app.
  • Try the tile in quick settings: swipe down from the top of the screen, tap the pencil/edit button, and drag the SoundPilot tile into your panel. Then tap it to switch profiles.

That's it. No special skills needed — the everyday stuff is exactly what we want tested.

How to tell us what you found

Write in the Telegram group “SoundPilot testers” — or message Sergey directly, whichever is easier. There's no required format. Useful things to mention:

  • your phone model and which headphones or speaker you use;
  • what you tried and whether it worked;
  • anything that broke, surprised you, or felt confusing — no detail is too small. “I didn't understand what this button does” is genuinely valuable feedback.

If the automatic switching suddenly stops working, definitely tell us and name your phone model — some phones put apps to sleep to save battery, and finding those cases is one of the main goals of this test.

Quick recap

  1. Join via the invitation link, install from Google Play.
  2. Set up two profiles and link your Bluetooth device.
  3. Use it daily for 14 days.
  4. Write anything you notice in the Telegram group.
  5. Don't leave the program until the test is over.

Thank you! 🎧
