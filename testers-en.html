What you're testing

SoundPilot is an Android app that handles your phone's sound for you. Two main things:

  1. A floating volume control — buttons on top of everything else, so you can change the volume without fumbling for the side buttons. Handy always, and especially if your volume button is worn out.
  2. Sound profiles that switch themselves — when Bluetooth headphones or a speaker connect, the phone turns itself up and silences interruptions; when they disconnect, everything goes back as it was.

What you need

  • Required: an Android phone — Android 8 or newer, so roughly any phone from 2018 onwards.
  • Required: your own Google account on that phone — the one you use for Google Play.
  • Nice to have, not required: Bluetooth earbuds, a speaker, or a car stereo. Without one there's still plenty to test — you'll just skip the automatic part, and everything else works.

How to join — one time, 5 minutes

  1. Sergey will send you a personal invitation link.
  2. Open the link in a browser on your phone and tap “Become a tester”. Make sure you're signed in with your usual Google account.
  3. On the same page, follow the link to Google Play and install SoundPilot — just like any normal app.

One important rule: the test runs for 14 days, and Google requires every tester to stay in for the whole time. Please don't leave the testing program or uninstall the app until Sergey says the test is finished — otherwise your spot is lost and can't be recovered.

First setup — 5 minutes

  1. Open the app and allow what it asks for (access to Do Not Disturb, Bluetooth, drawing over other apps). Without these permissions it simply can't do its job.
  2. Create two sound profiles — for example “Normal” (your everyday settings) and “Headphones” (louder, calls silenced).
  3. Turn on the floating volume control and drag it wherever suits you — it snaps to the edge of the screen.
  4. If you have headphones or a speaker, link them to a profile: pick the device in the app and choose which profile should turn on when it connects.

What to do during the 14 days

Just use it as part of your normal life — ideally a little every day.

Everyone, with or without headphones:

  • Use the floating volume control instead of your phone's buttons. Move it around, try different spots, and see whether it gets in the way in games, video, or on a call.
  • Switch profiles manually from the app.
  • Try the quick-settings tiles: swipe down from the top of the screen, tap the pencil/edit button, and drag the SoundPilot tiles into your panel — one switches profiles, the other turns the floating control on and off.

If you have Bluetooth headphones or a speaker:

  • Connect and disconnect them the way you always do. Check that the profile switches on by itself and switches back when you disconnect.

That's it. No special skills needed — the everyday stuff is exactly what we want tested.

How to tell us what you found

Write in the Telegram group “SoundPilot testers” — or message Sergey directly, whichever is easier. There's no required format. Useful things to mention:

  • your phone model (and which headphones or speaker you use, if any);
  • what you tried and whether it worked;
  • anything that broke, surprised you, or felt confusing — no detail is too small. “I didn't understand what this button does” is genuinely valuable feedback.

If the automatic switching suddenly stops working, definitely tell us and name your phone model — some phones put apps to sleep to save battery, and finding those cases is one of the main goals of this test.

Quick recap

  1. Join via the invitation link, install from Google Play.
  2. Set up two profiles, turn on the floating volume control, link your Bluetooth device if you have one.
  3. Use it daily for 14 days.
  4. Write anything you notice in the Telegram group.
  5. Don't leave the program until the test is over.

Thank you! 🎧
